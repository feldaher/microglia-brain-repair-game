Close the Wound.

A needle has gone through the brain of a young zebrafish. In a day the wound will be shut: not by new cells, but by the brain's immune cells, the microglia, pulling the tissue together. You are one of them.

  • CrawlClick the tissue and your yellow cell goes there.
  • PullPress Space to hold on. The tissue creeps toward you.
  • CallPress C to bring in another microglia. One is not enough.
  • LookTwenty hours pass in a minute. Click a label for the real thing.
